About Me

Academic Focus & Future Direction

I am Yansen Liu (Eason), a high school student interested in Computer Science, Digital Art, and interdisciplinary applications of technology and creativity. My work in computer graphics and digital design reflects my fascination with how visual communication can be enhanced through technical skills.

Through years of basketball and music performance, I've developed discipline, teamwork, and the ability to perform under pressure—qualities that directly support my academic pursuits and collaborative projects. I hope to join student organizations focused on technology, arts, and community service, while building projects that combine my technical and creative interests. I'm particularly interested in how computing can enhance visual storytelling and user experience design.
